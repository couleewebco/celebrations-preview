Court Yard All-Inclusive Wedding Venue - Up to 325 Guests

Prepare to be captivated by the breathtaking elegance of the Court Yard, where black and white colors showcase a timeless look and provide the perfect backdrop for you to paint your dream wedding. Crystal chandeliers, custom LED lighting and beautiful ceiling drapery create a truly unforgettable atmosphere that you and your guests will love. With two full-service bars, a stunning bridal suite, groom suite, and a private outdoor courtyard, every detail has been meticulously crafted for your dream wedding or special event.

Court Yard - Bridal Suite

The Court Yard's Bridal Suite boasts three hair/makeup vanities with oversized mirrors and tons of natural sunlight. It also features a full kitchenette, marble fireplace, comfy furniture, and overlooks the outdoor ceremony altar. Your love story starts here, at one of the best bridal suites in La Crosse, WI.

  • Private Ground Floor Suite

  • 3 Hair/Makeup Vanities

  • Oversized Mirrors

  • Full Kitchenette

  • TV and Sound System

  • Marble Fireplace

  • Custom LED Lighting

  • Guest Capacity: Up to 20+ Guests

Ceremony Altar
