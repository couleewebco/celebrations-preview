Welcome to Celebrations on the River! We are proud to be La Crosse, Wisconsin's premier riverside wedding, banquet, and special event venue. Our facility can accommodate up to 400 guests and offers in-house catering, full-service bars, beautiful decorations, and experienced Wedding/Event Planners to ensure your event is a success. Choose from three stunning venues: Center Court, River Park, or the Court Yard. When you compare our venue and prices, you will see why Celebrations is the perfect choice for your special occasion. Schedule a tour today to see for yourself and take a look at our open dates!
