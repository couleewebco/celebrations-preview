Katie & Luke Anderson | 5.8.2021

Katie & Luke Anderson 5.8.21

What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?

Creams/Whites. Simplicity, timeless, and elegance.

What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?

Timeless elegance.

Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?

Incredible venue and amazing staff!

What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?

Looking out at all of closest family and friends from the head table!

Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?

Take one moment to just stop and take it all in with each other and no distractions.

What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?

Enjoy the process, don’t stress about the planning because in the end it comes out great!

How did you manage your guest count?

Through Zola app.

Who were your vendors? (DJ, Flowers, Hair/Makeup, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?

Evoke Chancy for flowers because she’s one of the best in the state!

Dress was from Veras in Madison, WI because they have a large selection and take time to work with you.

Hair was done by Stacy Smithback out of Wausau because she specializes in updos.

DJ was Dave Bangert out of Richland Center because he was our DJ growing up together!

Dessert was Christy Adsit out of Richland Center.

Cheese boards were Meister Cheese out of Muscoda, WI.

Photography, Quantaya Grapes Portraits.
