Design The Room Any Way You Want It

Theater

Rows of chairs (no tables) arranged so that all participants are facing towards the front of the room or stage.

Classroom

Rows of rectangular tables with chairs arranged so that all participants are facing towards the front of the room.

U-Shape

Rectangular tables arranged in a large U with open space in the middle. Participants are seated around the outside facing the center of the U.

Standing

Large open space with smaller cocktail tables throughout the room, with banquet tables along the outside perimeter for appetizers, registration, gifts, etc.

Banquet

Round tables arranged throughout the room with participants seated around the entire perimeter of each table. Each table seats 8-10 guests each.
