Paige & Heath Larsen | 9.25.2021

Paige & Heath Larsen 8.28.20

What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?

Navy, Charcoal Grey, burgundy, copper rose gold, dusty rose. The perfect fall colors.

What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?

Fun, relax, celebrating vibe!

Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?

It’s a beautiful place and I grew up on the River. So always wanted an outdoor wedding near the river.

What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?

Walking down the aisle and seeing all the guest that are their to support you and your husband. It’s was pure love and joy.

Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?

I would not! Everything went perfect!

What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?

The seating chart. Guest cancel the week of until the morning of the wedding. People can figure out where to sit.

What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?

Have fun. It’s a stressful time planning but the wedding day is going to go how it suppose to and it is out of your control no matter how much planning you do!

How did you manage your guest count?

I used the The knot app. Great tool.

Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Flowers, Hair/Makeup, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?

Music Mix of La Crosse- DJ

Cindy Wedding Designs- Flowers

Alder & Root Photography – Photography

Hair Haven – Hair

Brooke Mootz- Makeup

Peace Oven Cakes – Dessert

Hoffland Films – Videography
