When the ceremony has just finished and the night’s reception is about to begin, what are your guests doing? Are they bored and impatiently waiting for the reception to begin? Or are they relaxed catching up over delicious appetizers and mixed drinks? Well if you have fun appetizers your guests will have a wonderful and enjoyable time waiting for the reception to start.

**Charcuterie Board**

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/5ZWdFlHufiiDHPAAMErXr6N5cY7ULi-ecE0UVrTkCO3bIHxSVsbX6MIjMx35j_U6O-PHTX_Ngc03e00ryGRk4wiHJr_fGO9OosFklDDrB2UuYPHq9u7FkAzOLKgR_uRPqr-OvW8tU_7izAo5zQRUvXA)

A charcuterie board is a great way to present delicious food in a beautiful and colorful way. All your guests will love to have lots of options to choose from.

**Skewers**

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/RWt_YRId5dLafqmn39gq2plqtRyluH3c7gmq2vf-eMPt_ShAgOy-lNAmkCSzNanKGpyd5CXjTp2gMXyK0rqRAHLoLxKp_Dm02J_RDEjgYQFJJfbb9ZAfI95kgDUbvwrEgh2IfI-ZiwgH44ss3LImIBQ)
![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/0TAhfTlmR-EpF96T9gHbWkJ6jcmvWER1PGZEzzP2Rpqfdx0UdVEpMlAxc96tsgA6oNlSscYMhQCuCXlueQxgxi3fRt2YIumU6rMA9GMD9XgFqQ6UR3vYDblR74vRw3_JZvXyoTmqOyBRzNZtLM0y2QE)

Skewers provide fun flavors and combinations to please everyone’s pallet.

**BBQ Meatballs**

![](https://lh6.googleusercontent.com/is5Eo-pl9C1zDBJcQVGevRN0Wth4eAWwmyGSYd7UjtrvAEflsTu9uG0os4YAApMptePL1Et_FdXD-ILY-laM76l3QctvHpcZ87q_fOwfiMa4789ewHuiSGO0_bpAbBC84sWnKIucfJqT4hNkvxlNWwg)

Meatballs are a simple savory delicious option to treat your guests with before dinner

**Italian Bruschetta**

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/zMboYoJsUMSMC29vF-N5yiCdA-x-jyg5KvU-Mv7vVUUHCohscsorYDrvzoTGo3TVM-8YSprtA4Ua0AFnOZV9ulyltayYd2qqYQW_2IDHojjORSwURVuL8_L-pszcM9SaE342m33ZldqUntAkJ0MhPRM)

Italian bruschetta is a delicious option for whatever occasion but especially a wedding for people that love italian cuisine

**Bacon Wrapped…**

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/ccMOQbFWqpaL3PKiIjaluvkuSLLEF_x3H9CjfE3XALl97z0xUKlmG00NdVAZj2vPGajqpGrzS3oWJgqRd-ETSauBMj5ZBK7BmPnN2IO9xPeMN5KaqT55OF-q_pY2szG89n2OqKyN7BSeX_wsIO0ooYU)

It is definitely a long lasting trend to bacon wrap foods for wedding appetizers. There are just so many options; bacon wrapped shrimp, bacon wrapped scallops, bacon wrapped cream cheese. What can’t be bacon wrapped?

**Stuffed Mushrooms**

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/yUa5sVbqI2gIYHzPuxe-g10yx2EOmLXkzRSuQPWxVz6GwHVYYaMrAKEMnptwf0qGIG3nAIzz0_2jVNq0C8k9O2ZT5znlav2Hhif1tcOrDGig83CQCM0HBQf1H3gYDGlDq6gpY4p-irxiDyvdPZQhUqw)

Stuffed mushrooms are a delicious option that is an extremely popular choice for a wedding appetizer.

**Deviled Eggs**

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/-fo1eSndGegqWEKHL0Vcz4psxdbOx7hy86xWSv7CNCQfng9z7Mv_tdBnhAMobbYjp02DGe6ABBczb_-HA78UdlU2ZYGExCBYu3yj2DQxVTDOx5QclIKdtFOm2ISbaTy4YdBJg_1IZ5YFhWChDRk5d0o)

The best thing about appetizers is that they can be whatever you’d like them to be. Whether sweet or savory, lots of flavors or one there are so many different appetizers that are all wildly different but still wildly popular!
