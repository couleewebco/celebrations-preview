Loaded with savings and uncompromising value, the VIP Photographer Package is exclusively available to Celebrations' couples from La Crosse's finest photographers.

### **Package Includes:**

- **Kick-Off Consultation** – 30 minute consultation to discuss your big day, how things work, and what’s most important to you.
- **Engagement Session** - 1 hour photo shoot with 5 fully edited digital pictures of your choice, that you can use anyway you like, delivered within 7 days of your engagement session.
- **Wedding Day Coverage** - 8 hours of continuous wedding-day coverage, by a single shooter, with a minimum of 25 fully edited digital pictures of your choice, that you can use anyway you like, delivered within 30 days of your wedding.
- **Photo Album** – A minimum of 250 wedding day digital picture proofs to choose from, within 45 calendar days of your wedding. Additional costs may apply, however some VIP Vendors often go above and beyond at no additional cost.
