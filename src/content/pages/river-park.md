# River Park - 3 Seasons Room

The 3 Seasons Room is sure to spark ideas for a fabulous and sunny event with its floor-to-ceiling windows, vaulted ceilings, and stunning river views. With seating for up to 100 guests, it's the ideal spot for your event.

# River Park - Ceremony Altar

River Park features a beautiful outdoor wedding altar overlooking the river for you to tie the knot. Located just a few feet from the river, surrounded by woods, River Park's outdoor ceremony altar provides the perfect stage to say "I do".

- 20′ X 10′ Raised Gazebo with Glass Railings
- Elegant Overhead Sun Shades
- White Ceremony Chairs
- Wireless Microphone & Outdoor Speakers

- Picture-Perfect Views
- Several Backup Plans in Case of Inclement Weather
- Guest Capacity: 200 Seated

# River Park - Bridal Suite

Enjoy your 1st class bridal suite where you can get ready, get your hair/makeup done, sip on some drinks and snap a few pictures. Makeup vanities, dress hooks, full height mirrors, and tons of natural light provides an incredible experience before saying your vows.

- Three Hair/Makeup Vanities
- Custom Furniture and Décor
- Bridesmaids Lockers

- Kitchenette
- TV and Sound System
- Climate Control

- Custom LED Lighting
- Square Feet: 400+ square feet
- Guest Capacity: Up to 15+ Guests

# Outdoor Patio

Few venues can match the ambiance of River Park's outdoor patio overlooking the river. Adjacent to the main entrance and 3 Seasons Room the outdoor patio seats 50+ guests, offers amazing views of the water and a one-of-a-kind atmosphere.

- Gorgeous Riverside Views
- State-of-the-Art Audio System
- Wrought-Iron Outdoor Tables & Chairs

- LED Lighting on Trees
- Outdoor Gas Fire Pit
- Access to Boat Docks
