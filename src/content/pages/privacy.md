**DRAFT – client to approve**

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Shenanigans of Wisconsin LLC DBA Celebrations on the River ("Celebrations," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website at [https://celebrationslacrosse.com/](/) (and its preview). This draft describes how we collect and use information on this website. It is **not final** until Celebrations approves it.

This policy covers only this website and the forms on it. It does not cover in-person tours, contracts, payment processing, or other tools you may use after you book (for example, a signing or payment link sent separately).

**1. Information you give us through website forms**

We collect only what you choose to submit. Depending on the form, that may include:

- Name, email address, and phone number, and (on tour forms) your partner’s name
- Preferred contact method, and whether you opt in to text messages
- Event or wedding details, such as event type, dates or months of interest, days of the week, guest count, and which venues you want to see
- Requested tour dates and times, and how you heard about us
- Notes or questions you write in
- Business or event quote details, such as company name, start and end times, and food, drink, décor, or audio/visual preferences
- Taste-test RSVP details, including the event date, your wedding date, and number of guests
- Career-inquiry details, such as the positions and hours you are interested in, when you could start, and interview availability
- Wedding cost calculator “Share Quote” submissions, including your contact information and the itemized estimate of the options you selected

This website does not create user accounts, and it does not collect login passwords or payment card numbers.

**2. Information collected automatically**

When you visit the site, our hosting provider may record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Some pages may embed Google Maps or, if you choose to play a video, a YouTube player. Those services have their own privacy policies.

**3. How we use this information**

We use form submissions to respond to your inquiry, schedule a tour, share or confirm a quote, take a taste-test RSVP, or review a job inquiry. If you check the text-message opt-in, we may text you about that inquiry, tour scheduling, appointments, and event planning. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

**4. Who we share it with**

We share information only as needed to run the website and respond to you, including:

- Our venue staff who handle inquiries, tours, quotes, and hiring
- Service providers that deliver form messages (our form processor and email delivery provider)
- A text-delivery provider, only if you opted in and only to send you those messages
- When the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our guests and staff

**5. How long we keep it**

We keep inquiry and planning information as long as it is needed to respond to you, plan an event, consider a job application, or meet legal or record-keeping requirements, then delete or de-identify it when it is no longer needed.

**6. Your choices and rights**

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold, or withdraw text-message consent (reply STOP, or contact us). We will honor requests as required by applicable law. Email [events@celebrationslacrosse.com](mailto:events@celebrationslacrosse.com) or call (608) 783-3335.

**7. Children’s privacy**

This website is meant for adults planning events or seeking work. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

**8. Changes**

We will post updates on this page and change the date above when this policy is approved or revised.
