# Join the Team Behind the Magic

At Celebrations on the River, we don’t just host events; we celebrations milestones. From the first "I do" to the final toast of a corporate gala, our team is the heartbeat of every celebration. We believe that behind every breathtaking moment is a group of passionate, detail-oriented professionals dedicated to excellence.

If you have a heart for hospitality, a sharp eye for detail, and a drive to create unforgettable experiences, we want to meet you.

## Our Core Values

Behind every breathtaking moment is a team that cares more, notices more, and gives more—because we know that for our clients, this isn’t just an event. It's their cherished memories.

![Core Values at Celebrations on the River](/media/2025/12/Core-Values_Final.jpg)

## What We Look For

We hire for **attitude** and train for **skill**. While experience in hospitality is a plus, our ideal teammates share these core values:

- **Radical Hospitality:** A genuine desire to serve and anticipate guest needs.
- **Poise Under Pressure:** The ability to stay calm and kind when the "big moment" arrives.
- **Attention to Detail:** You notice the tilted centerpiece or the smudge on a glass before anyone else does.
- **Collaboration:** A "no task is too small" mindset—we all help each other cross the finish line.

## Why Build Your Future With Us?

- **A Culture of Celebration:** We work hard, but we work in an environment filled with joy, love, and success.
- **Professional Growth:** Whether you’re starting in service or leading event operations, we provide the experience and mentorship to help you level up your career.
- **Leading Venue:** We are Southwestern Wisconsin’s premier destination for weddings and large-scale events, hosting over 150+ events annually with three gorgeous riverside venues, accommodating up to 1,000 guests simultaneously.
- **Stunning Environment:** Trade the cubicle for celebrating love and happiness in one of our three gorgeous riverside venues.
- **Team Impact:** We are a tight-knit family. Your voice is heard, and your contribution is visible in every happy client’s smile.
