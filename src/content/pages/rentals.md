# Decor Rental FAQ's

##### Is it Really Unlimited Decor?

Yes. But with some rules...

1. You have to choose from Celebrations' in-house decor options published on our website.
2. All decor is reserved under a first come, first serve basis.
3. Your final deadline to cancel decor reservations without penalty is 90 days prior to your wedding day. If you cancel within 90 days, you will be charged menu price.
4. Unlimited decor is available exclusively to Celebrations' couples with new reservations booked after 11/22/2024, no exceptions.

More questions? Speak with a Celebrations' wedding planner.

##### Can I rent Unlimited Decor if my event is not at Celebrations?

Sorry, unlimited decor is only available if you're hosting your event at Celebrations.

##### How long do I get to keep my rental(s)?

You select the date and time you want to pick up and return your rental item(s). The price and length of the rental is shown for each item in the shopping cart.

##### Where is Celebrations décor rental located?

Our address is 2100 Dawson Avenue in La Crosse, WI 54603. Go to the south end of the building where you will see a bright blue door. Then call (608)-783-3335 and someone will meet you at the door.

##### Who do I contact with questions?

Please send an email to [events@celebrationslacrosse.com](mailto:events@celebrationslacrosse.com), call [(608)-783-3335](tel:+16087833335) or [contact us online.](/contact-us/)

##### I can't find what I'm looking for...or there's not enough. Will you buy it for me?

Depending on the item(s), we may consider it. It depends on the item(s), color(s), popularity, price and other considerations. Please send an email to [events@celebrationslacrosse.com](mailto:events@celebrationslacrosse.com), call [(608)-783-3335](tel:+16087833335) or [contact us online.](/contact-us/)

##### How do I place my order?

All orders must be submitted online through the shopping cart above.

##### Do you rent decor outside of Celebrations?

Yes. All décor is available to rent by anyone, at any location.

##### Is delivery available?

Yes. Delivery is available with a base fee of $100 + $4/per mile, up to 125 miles.

##### Is setup available?

You are responsible for all setup and cleanup. We do offer setup and cleanup, at an additional cost.

##### What happens if I break/damage something?

If damage occurs during your rental, you will be charged to repair or replace it. If something is broke, missing or unfix-able, you are charged the replacement fee. The replacement costs is listed under each rental item.

##### When can I pick up my décor?

You select the date and time you want to pick up your rental item(s) when you are placing your order online.
