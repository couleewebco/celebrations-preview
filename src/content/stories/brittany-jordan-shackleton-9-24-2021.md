![A woman and child in a boat on the water.](/media/2022/08/admin-2.jpg)

**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Cabernet, Dusty Rose, Dark Grey, and Ivory. Choose those colors because of a picture I had seen and fell in love.

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**
It was by the water. We are very active on the water in the summer months and on vacations so it had a special meaning to us.

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**
Seeing my now husband cry on our first look.

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

The rain! If only it didn’t rain for that two hours during the ceremony.

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

People’s meal choices. They seemed to forget what they wanted anyway.

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

Keep all receipts and documentation over any interactions. We were very fortunate with all vendors we worked with, but have heard horror stories.

**How did you manage your guest count?**

Through The Knot wedding website.

**Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Flowers, Hair/Makeup, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?**

DJ: Music Mix of La Crosse

Hair: KA Salon

Makeup: Angie’s Exclusive Beauty Bar

Dessert: Linda’s Bakery

Floral: Cottage Garden Floral and Perennial Petals by Arrie

Dress: Charlotte’s Bridal and Formal Wear

Suits: Men’s Warehouse

Photographer: Sarah Schultz Photography

We choose our vendors mainly by word of mouth and my own experience with them. Also, based on responsiveness which was very important to me.
