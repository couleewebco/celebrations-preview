**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Gold sage and white. For a clean simple look.

**What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?**

Inspiration came by just wanting to have fun and not worry about details.

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

Price!

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Reading our vows to each other privately.

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

I would have chosen a larger, more colorful bouquet, and a different dress.

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

The music, I don’t even remember hearing it during our ceremony.

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

People don’t care about what the tables look like.

**How did you manage your guest count?**

We’re anti-social people.

**Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Flowers, Hair/Makeup, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?**

**DJ**– Midwest Sound because of price

**Photographer**– Jessica Radke because of friends referral

**Flowers**– Guenthers Flowers because that’s where he always gets them from

**Hair/Makeup**– Metropolitan Salon because they were the only salon open on Sundays Dessert- Linda’s Bakery & Eileen’s Cookies because they Re both our favorites
