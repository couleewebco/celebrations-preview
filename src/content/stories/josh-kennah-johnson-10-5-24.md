**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Terracotta and Sage, I love all things fall but didn’t want the wedding to feel like Halloween

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Having my father and now husband be the first ones to see me in my dress

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2024/10/1AZ7A8357-copy.jpg)

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their wedding?**

Hear others ideas and opinions but DO NOT let them take over or decide what YOU want for your own wedding!!
