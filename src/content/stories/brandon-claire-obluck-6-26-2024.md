Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Hair/Makeup, Florist, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?

- DJ: Music in Motion (Tim) – a family friend – he went above and beyond for us!
- Photographer/Videographer: Madeline Litler and Matt Ayer – we booked through Encompass, but these two both have their own photography and videography businesses as well. They were so friendly, knowledgeable, and produced incredible pictures and video!
- Hair: Kristin Taggert – heard of her through a friend – she knows what she is doing and her hair holds longer than others we have experienced.
- Makeup: Amber Rae – recommended by our hair stylist
- Florist: I did my own flowers (wood/fake) – I don’t love flowers so I did not want to waste money on real ones, or having someone else do them.
- Dessert: Celebrations for the couples cake, and Sams Club for the cookies/brownies – it came down to our preferences of dessert, cost, etc.
