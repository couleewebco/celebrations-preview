**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Walking down the isle to Michael.

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

It was such a perfect day. I only wish I had gotten the chance to talk to all our guests.

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

I was very concerned about our timeline, and while it does sort of matter, it ended up being totally fine.

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2024/10/IMG_1674-Karina-Laufenberg.jpg)

**Why did you host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

I’ve always been obsessed with Celebrations! The decor of River Park just felt like Michael and I. Plus, a venue that handles basically everything made planning so easy.

**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Cabernet, Terracotta, Cream, Sage and Dark Green – I chose them because they felt like fall.

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their wedding?**

You can keep it simple and still have the most beautiful day.

**How did you manage your guest count?**

We set our budget first, then built our guest list, THEN gave our parents a set amount they could include with very clear boundaries.

**Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Hair/Makeup, Florist, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?**

- Photographer: Alder and Root Photography
- DJ: Ever After Entertainment
- Makeup: by Sarah Koblitz
- Flowers: Viroqua Floral Mercantile
- Cake: Meringue Bakery + Kwik Trip Bakery Glazers

A lot of these were suggestions from friends or Celebrations. I’ve known Sarah Koblitz since high school so that was an easy pick for make up. Desserts also were easy – who doesn’t love a Kwik Trip Glazer? Our couples cake from Meringue was mainly my mom’s idea, and the owner of that bakery is their neighbor.
