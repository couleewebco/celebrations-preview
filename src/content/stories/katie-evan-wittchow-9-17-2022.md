**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Navy & maroon. Moody and our favorite colors

**What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?**

We love fall in La Crosse. The colors are our colors

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

We toured a lot of places in our hometowns and current towns, and La Crosse (our college town) as well. But when we drove into Celebrations, it was a completely different vibe. We knew the second we walked in, we were getting married there. There’s no words to describe “why”

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Our wedding party’s speeches

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

Have a deep green accenting instead of maroon

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

The amount of people on the list

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

It’s YOUR wedding, no one else’s opinions matter, do what makes YOU happy

**How did you manage your guest count?**

Knot.com

DJ – Complete

Hair & Makeup – Orange Pearl Salon

Dessert – Celebrations
