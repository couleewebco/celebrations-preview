**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Blush, Navy, Greenery. We chose these colors because we wanted something classy, elegant and timeless.

**What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?**

We wanted our wedding look to be simple, modern, and elegant. We wanted colors that complimented the summer greenery and neutral colors.

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

From the start, Celebrations was accommodating and willing to work with us. We had a few requests and they made them happen. It was also nice to have so much already included in the VIP packages. It made the planning process so easy. Ann was also amazing from the very beginning.

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Our favorite moment of our wedding day was at the ceremony. It is a special feeling to walk down the aisle and see everyone that you love there to celebrate a new beginning.

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

Honestly, no. Our day was perfect the way it was even if every part didn’t go exactly as planned. Most things that didn’t go as planned are things that we laugh about now and are just special memories.

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

Dessert.

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2022/12/IMG_2594_-_Delaney_Colbert_1_1760x1760.jpg)

### [Delaney & Scott Taylor 6.25.22](/delaney-scott-taylor-6-25-2022/)

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

Before the wedding: Most things aren’t worth stressing over. There will be things that don’t work out and that is ok.

Day of the wedding: Be in the moment. It can be so easy to get caught up in what is going to happen next or if something isn’t going exactly as planned. The day goes by so fast. Your vendors and wedding party are there to help make sure things go smoothly. Let them take care of it so you can truly take everything in and enjoy the day. Also, during the reception, take time to step away from the party and be with each other and take it all in.

**How did you manage your guest count?**

We decided where we wanted to cut it off with family and then kept it to closest friends.

**Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Flowers, Hair/Makeup, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?**

DJ: Ever After Entertainment

Photographer: Emily Jean Photography

Videographer: Morrison Media LLC

Flowers: Cindy’s Wedding Designs

Hair: Blondes and Bridals

Makeup: Makeup by Brooke Weber

We chose our vendors through both Celebrations VIP packages as well as our own research and connections.
