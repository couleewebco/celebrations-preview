**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Our color palette was dusty purples with burgundy suits, magenta, dark greens and gold. I chose these colors because I love purple and the romantic tone of all the colors together was very ethereal and fairytale-esque which is what I was going for. It turned out amazing and really complimented the beautiful room we were in!

**What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?**

Beauty in the Beast and wanting something classy but different

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

I instantly fell in love with the Center Court venue and the versatility of the venue – had both indoor and outdoor capability, a beautiful dining room, the tiki bar and outdoor patio and the large ceremony site. Plus all the on-site support! Ann is amazing!

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

How can I choose? My favorite moment by far was seeing my now husband watching me walk down the aisle! It was a moment I won’t forget. I loved getting ready with my best friends, and we partied the night away. It was amazing and I am so grateful for the support we had from your team!

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

I would not do family style dining. We had a lot of complaints after the wedding about people not getting enough to eat. It seemed like tables did not get the same amount of food served to them – some had too much, some too little. I wish we would have done buffet.

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

Centerpieces and name cards. I wish I would’ve done else decor and more flowers because Shanna at Evoke Chancy KILLED IT!

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

Do it together. It is such a special time and should be a reflection of you both. Also: SOAK IT UP. Everyone says it goes by so fast and it is so true.
