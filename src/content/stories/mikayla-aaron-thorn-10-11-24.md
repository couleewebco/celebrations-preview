**Why did you host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?Â**

The venue is so elegant and reasonably priced with food and drinks included with the venue. We also loved that they had a preferred vendors page on their website which made the planning process smooth.

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Our ceremony with our closest friends and family, grand entrance, and our first dance

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

We’d probably change our wing bar being correct (which we’ve resolved)

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2024/11/IMG_9863-Mikayla-Baker.jpeg)

**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Burgundy with sage green; the bride’s favorite color is burgundy and loved the greenery look of the sage green

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?Â**

Decorations

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their wedding?**

Do NOT sweat the small stuff. It really doesn’t matter. Focus on the moments with your partner.

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2024/11/IMG_9868-Mikayla-Baker.jpeg)

**How did you manage your guest count?Â**

We didn’t want anything too big so we decided our max to invite was 200 (100 each) and ended up with 130 who attended.

Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Hair/Makeup, Florist, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?

- DJ – DJ Brettly
- Photography – Alder and Root
- Hair & Makeup – Salon SoLo
- Florist – Solawood flowers (bridesmaids) + Maple Grove Floral (bride)
- Bride’s Dress: The Wedding Tree

We found the DJ, Photographer, and hair/makeup from the celebrations VIP list on their website and chose the ones that stood out with reasonable price.
