**Why did you host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

I liked that they handled everything! It was nice not to have to worry about multiple vendors.

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

I have a lot of favorite moments of my wedding day but one of them is having a first dance with my grandpa to the song he used to sing to me as a little girl. It was so special and I don’t think there was a dry eye in the building!

**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Dessert Rose and Beige. We wanted something with a BoHo fall theme.

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2024/10/195DFE1E-9F6C-45BF-A051-DEC749D7BC04-Kalyn-Gloede.jpeg)

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

I don’t think I would change anything. Everything worked out the way it was supposed to and went very smoothly.

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

I feel like I didn’t obsess over anything. It was such a smooth process that made planning so easy.

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2024/10/C95AF7EF-7CF3-45A8-BDB4-615C566C43EB-Kalyn-Gloede.jpeg)

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their wedding?**

To enjoy the day! It truly does go by so quickly.

**Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Hair/Makeup, Florist, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?**

- **Hair:** Kristi-Anna Knight
- **Makeup:** Kaylee Hammond
- **Dessert:** The pearl ice cream cart & Shoua Yang for cake
- **DJ:** Chris Mataska of Collins and Company
- **Flowers:** were made by the brides maid of honor/sister

It was very easy to choose vendors as I am in the bridal industry myself. Most of my vendors are either friends of mine or friends of friends. It’s definitely nice knowing others in the industry!
