### [Laura Wright & Brady Nelson 9.11.21](/laura-wright-brady-nelson-9-11-2021/)

**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Emerald green & gold (fall colors and Packers-adjacent)

**What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?**

We both love reading so many of our decorations were centered around books, mixed with some antique gold–kind of fairytale vibes when the flowers and greenery were mixed in.

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

La Crosse was centrally located for our wedding guests and it’s our home. Celebrations was an easy choice because Ann and her team were easy to get a long with upon first introduction and the layout was beautiful.

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

The dancing!

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

We got so busy during the reception we didn’t make time to go visit every table, I wish I could have talked to more guests!

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

Give yourself more time to be engaged, no one expects you to plan a wedding in less than a year.

**How did you manage your guest count?**

We used theknot.com and electronic RSVPs, it was much easier than dealing with paper responses.

**Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Flowers, Hair/Makeup, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?**

MegaMusic DJ

Hannah Marie Photography

The Cakery

We were primarily focused on our budget and all of our vendors did a fantastic job.
