**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Navy & Dusty Rose

**What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?**

Something that fit our style and nothing over the top

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

The location is beautiful and the benefit of a one stop shop is invaluable to busy brides and grooms

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Amanda: Our “alone time” on the picture paths

Nick: Amanda saying her special message to the kids

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

Realize that some of the stress could have went away trusting in what Celebrations did for us

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

Desserts

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

Block out the background noise and focus on what you want to do, what you want to have.

**How did you manage your guest count?**

Guest manager on The Knot website
