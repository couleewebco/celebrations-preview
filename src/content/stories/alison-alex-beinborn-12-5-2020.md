**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Gold, navy, and light blue – Went with the season.

**What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?**

Holiday / Winter Feel

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

Wonderful staff and accommodations. There was not one thing that they were not able to do that we wanted. They were on top of everything and made the day stress free and wonderful.

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Hard to pick just one. The entire day was beyond what we could have imagined.

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

NO!

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

The small details. No one but us would remember which center pieces we had.

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

Use this venue! It took much of the stress of planning away including having to find/make decorations, set up, clean up, etc. They even had “shout” wipes when my flowers bled onto my dress.

**How did you manage your guest count?**

Wonderful especially with the COVID-19 pandemic.

**Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Flowers, Hair/Makeup, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?**

DJ K.C. Entertainment – Great reviews from other friends and his light show is amazing.

Flowers – Viroqua Village Flower Market. Very personable and reasonably priced. They made bouquets for me out of fake flowers I already purchased.

Photos – V imagery + design; Basic internet search but glad we went with him. He was very personable and made everything run smoothly. The pictures were AMAZING.

Dessert – Family Friend

Makeup – Dani Malay. She was wonderful. A coworker of mine used her for her wedding.
