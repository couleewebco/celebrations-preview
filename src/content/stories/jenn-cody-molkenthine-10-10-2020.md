### [Jenn & Cody Molkenthine 10.10.20](/jenn-cody-molkenthine-10-10-2020/)

**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Burnt orange, and Plum purple. Orange has always been my favorite color and my husband’s has always been purple. We agreed they worked really well as fall colors and why not use our favorites.

**What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?**

Fall, and semi elegant. I just really wanted orange in my wedding and thankfully my husband agreed. Pinterest and Ann were really the ones who pulled it all together.

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

It was a one stop shop! Ann took such good care of us. I loved having it all in one spot.

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

There were so many! Just walking into the hall in the morning and seeing it all out together. Walking down the aisle and seeing my soon to be husband for the first time and also after the grand March and the party.

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

Not do it during Covid times. A lot of people weren’t able to make it and I wish more people could have enjoyed our day with us.

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

My flowers and florist.

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

Trust your wedding planners, they make your vision come together.

**How did you manage your guest count?**

Covid helped with that.
