**1. What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?**

We wanted a very bright and airy summertime vibe.

**2. What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

 We focused a lot on greenery with white floral with some blush and

champagne accents. We chose the colors because they complimented the look and feel of the venue.

**3. Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

It’s hard to find a venue that’s bright, modern, and close to home.

We also were very drawn to the outdoor area, especially at night!

We also loved that Celebrations is full service and we didn’t have to

outsource any of our vendors.

**4. What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Seeing everything come to life after years of planning and watching

our friends and family have the best night.

**5. Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

No! It truly was the perfect day.

**6. What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

Our timeline. We had everything planned out in 15 minute

increments from the time we got there until the reception events

were over, but things flowed the way they were supposed to and

everything went smoothly.

**7. What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

 Don’t worry about the small stuff. Your groomsmen might arrive at

the wedding with the wrong color suit, your DJ might play the wrong

song- but at the end of the day, things work themselves out and

you’ll laugh about the bumps in the road!
