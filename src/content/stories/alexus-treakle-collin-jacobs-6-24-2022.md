**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Neptune, Dusty blue, and Blushing Pink

**What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?**

Beach themed

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

We were always told the sky was the limit which made our vision for our perfect day come true. Everyone was so friendly and the VIP package made it stress free leading up to our big day!

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2022/12/310656129_5518809248233725_998150011532584931_n.jpg)

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Our favorite moment of our wedding day was having our Maid of Honor pray with us in front of our memorial table before we walked down the aisle and honoring those who have passed away by dancing a memorial dance.

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

I would of included an aisle runner so my veil wouldn’t of stuck to the cement.

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

The candy and photo book tables that we set up.

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2022/12/309815970_5518767751571208_1890808694561028563_n.jpg)

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

To remind yourself at the end of the day your wedding will be perfect because you got to marry the person whom you will love for the rest of your life.

**How did you manage your guest count?**

We started with immediate family. Then we included close family friends and our other friends. From there it was our coworkers.

**Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Flowers, Hair/Makeup, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?**

- DJ: DJ Brettly
