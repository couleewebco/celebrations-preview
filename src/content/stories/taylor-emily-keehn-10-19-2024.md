**Why did you host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?Â**

It was a beautiful wedding venue that suited our needs perfectly! When we walked in the River Park Room we both started tearing up as we knew it was the one! The staff was so kind and ensured that our day was special in every way! The simple fact that we did not need to plan decorations made a huge difference as well!

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Getting to marry my best friend!

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

No everything was perfect!

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2024/11/Sneak-Peek-557_Original-Emily-Keehn.jpeg)

**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Terracotta, not rust and not red!!

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their wedding?**

Make the day about you and no one else! You are the ones getting married not your guests!

**How did you manage your guest count?Â**

We determined who was most important to us, family or not, and built the list from there. If we did not know a significant other they were not invited and if we did not know the children of a guest they were not invited.

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2024/11/Sneak-Peek-556_Original-Emily-Keehn.jpeg)
