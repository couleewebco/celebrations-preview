**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Silver and dark blue. It fit our winter theme very well.

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/-HivpxC-5Qcz-62ti6oOVjDjSi1nFY6XkFEXXSoTcdPkfRxrGhAZGFfNM4cipCVU0s0Ig6EhR3CPb4FgDSqq-Bz6IhvTzWeWSEeaKYLXqPKxPHFuyTwcrXhJxGEobfI9eS1c_68LTyjwLc7vRh3GQB3nn-rxd4iq8ce4pj0Rl39khvYcBAbyOs4NUXWEkQ)

**What was the inspiration for the look and feel of your wedding?**

We wanted a winter wedding, and built our color palette and decoration themes from there.

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

We loved the hall and thought it would fit perfectly with our winter themes and color palette.

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

The dinner was our favorite part.

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

We carefully planned and loved every detail in our wedding and would not have changed any of it.

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/zBpMnuFfd0nI-9kYl0rv6oHbkRVAyMPCTkKPIjdhRgsgX-rCIHFLmFUoBjwmKL4Lu_n4tclYZgr681i6U_hEHZDk9rCCNQKInEiJcju0GUKqoOY90u-8tHMjroLj38gRP4k_c8hBjmKE_R-u5ulLpOiduYf86zsi0vm9mSCPOD8czTJ_9qlRnAx1QQLozw)

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

We split up our tasks for the wedding based on who cares most about the task, but also kept each other calm and from spiraling over the details.

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

Be honest about what you care most about in the planning and be willing to compromise where you don’t.

**How did you manage your guest count?**

We kept close family and friends and followed up with most of the guests regarding their ability to come.

**Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Flowers, Hair/Makeup, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?**

Complete wedding photos and DJ. We found them through Celebration’s most common vendors, and we loved them.
