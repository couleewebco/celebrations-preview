**What was your color palette? Why did you choose those colors?**

Dusty Blue, Navy, greenery, white, These are our favorite colors!

**Why did you choose to host your wedding at Celebrations on the River?**

We loved that it was essentially a one stop shop and that it was right by the water!

**What was your favorite moment of your wedding day?**

Our Reception!

**Would you change anything if you could do it all over again?**

I would not change a thing!

![A green background with the letter e in grey.](/media/2022/12/6F82C18F-03DF-40F4-B332-39A900B5D872_-_Taylor_Miller_1760x1760.jpeg)

### [Taylor & Leo Judd-Miller 9.3.22](/taylor-leo-judd-miller-9-3-2022/)

**What was one thing you obsessed over, but realized it did not matter?**

Flowers! It took me 1.5 years to decide on every single flower that were part of our arrangements

**What advice do you have for future couples planning their weddings?**

Trust your wedding coordinators! It was so nice to be able to count on them to ensure the day ran smoothly.

**Who were your vendors? (DJ, Photographer, Flowers, Hair/Makeup, Dessert, etc.) How did you choose them?**

- Photographer: Volkman Photography
- Flowers: Cottage Garden Floral
- DJ: DJ Brettly
- Makeup: Brooke Weber
- Hair: JLS Studios
- Cake: Meringue Bakery Cupcake Artistry + Pearl ice cream cart
- Wedding Planner: Elegant Outcomes

All were very easy to choose – loved the work they all did and service they provided!
