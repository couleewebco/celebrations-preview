Wedding Decor Ideas | Decor Vision Boards

Vision Boards

The possibilities are endless. Our experienced and talented Wedding Planners will make your dream wedding come true without breaking the bank. These decor ideas to spark your wedding decor inspiration and help you create the perfect celebration. Here's just a few examples of gorgeous decor themes...

Boho Glam

Boho Glam blends free spirited elegance with luxurious accents. Think macramé table runners, pampas grass centerpieces, and gold-trimmed dinnerware. Add crystal chandeliers or vintage lanterns to bring in a touch of sophistication while keeping that laid back, romantic feel.

Boho

This effortless and earthy theme embraces natural elements and soft, dreamy textures. Layers of greenery, woven chargers, and candlelit tables create a relaxed yet enchanting atmosphere. Incorporate rattan décor, dried florals, and soft drapery for a truly bohemian vibe.

Classic and Timeless

A refined and elegant wedding style that never goes out of fashion. Crisp white linens, delicate floral arrangements, and polished silverware create a sophisticated ambiance. Whether you opt for grand chandeliers or candlelit centerpieces, this theme exudes luxury and grace.

Enchanted Forest

Step into a magical woodland setting with moss covered tables, twinkling fairy lights, and lush greenery. Wooden elements, delicate wildflowers, and floating candles enhance the dreamy atmosphere, making it the perfect backdrop for a storybook wedding.

Fairy Garden

Soft pastels, cascading florals, and twinkling string lights bring this whimsical theme to life. Floral arches, vintage furniture, and delicate lace details complete the romantic setting, offering wedding decor inspiration straight out of a fairytale.

Glitz and Glam

For couples who love sparkle and extravagance, this theme is all about dazzling details. Sequined tablecloths, tall floral centerpieces, and shimmering candle holders set the stage for a high end celebration. Gold accents and crystal embellishments bring a sense of grandeur.

Romance

Soft candlelight, delicate roses, and flowing fabrics create an intimate and dreamy wedding atmosphere. A palette of blush, ivory, and champagne enhances the love-filled setting, making every detail feel effortlessly romantic.

Rustic Chic

Rustic charm meets refined elegance in this cozy yet sophisticated theme. Wooden farm tables, burlap runners, and mason jar centerpieces provide a warm, welcoming feel, while elegant floral arrangements and twinkle lights add a touch of charm.

Summer Sunset

Inspired by golden hues and warm tones, this theme features sun kissed florals, terracotta accents, and glowing candlelight. Sunset colored blooms like dahlias and ranunculus paired with wooden or rattan décor create a dreamy summer celebration.

Dark and Moody

Dramatic and romantic, this theme embraces rich jewel tones, moody candlelight, and deep colored florals. Velvet textures, black tinted glassware, and gothic inspired décor bring an edgy yet sophisticated touch to your wedding aesthetic.

Festival Fall

Celebrate autumn’s beauty with a vibrant color palette of burnt orange, deep red, and golden yellow. Pumpkins, dried wheat, and rustic lanterns make the perfect table decor, while cozy plaid blankets add a charming seasonal touch.

Halloween

A Halloween themed wedding can be hauntingly beautiful with deep burgundy florals, black lace table runners, and candlelit candelabras. Gothic inspired centerpieces and dramatic backdrops bring mystery and magic to your special day.

Cheerfully Christmas

Celebrate the season with a cozy and festive wedding filled with holiday charm. Think twinkling lights, evergreen garlands, and bright red poinsettias. Classic plaid accents and seasonal treats like hot cocoa bars add warmth and nostalgia.

Red Christmas

A bold and glamorous take on holiday wedding decor, featuring deep red roses, rich velvet linens, and gold trimmed dinnerware. Pair with twinkling candlelight and festive greenery for an unforgettable winter celebration.

Winter Wonderland

Transform your wedding into a snowy dream with icy blue hues, silver accents, and frosted floral arrangements. Crystal centerpieces, twinkling fairy lights, and faux snow covered décor create a magical, wintery escape for your guests.
